Privacy Policy

Item: WhatsApp Web Contact & Group Exporter • Last Updated: August 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy describes how WhatsApp Web Contact & Group Exporter ("the extension") handles user information. We believe in total data ownership and zero tracking.

1. Zero Data Collection & External Transmission

  • No Remote Servers: The extension does not communicate with or operate any external servers, databases, or cloud APIs.
  • No Personal Data Transmission: We never collect, transmit, store, sell, or share your contacts, phone numbers, group names, chat messages, or metadata.
  • 100% Client-Side Execution: All extraction, deduplication, formatting, and file exports (CSV, Excel, vCard, ZIP) execute strictly within your local browser sandbox.

2. Local Data Processing

When you open the extension on WhatsApp Web (web.whatsapp.com), the following data is processed temporarily in memory solely to fulfill your requested export:

  • Contacts & Leads: Read from your active session to generate your export files.
  • Group Metadata: Participant numbers and admin roles read to create group spreadsheets.
  • Local Preferences: Theme settings, column configurations, and delimiters are stored in your own browser via chrome.storage.local and never leave your device.

3. Chrome Permissions

  • https://web.whatsapp.com/*: Required to read contact and group metadata on demand.
  • storage: Required to store your UI preferences locally on your computer.

4. Analytics & Tracking

We do not include any third-party analytics libraries, advertising trackers, tracking pixels, or remote telemetry scripts.
